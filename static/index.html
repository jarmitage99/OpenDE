OpenDE

An open-source reimplementation of the Deus Ex engine

About

OpenDE runs UnrealScript bytecode from the shipped packages. This page is the same engine compiled to WebAssembly with Emscripten, using WebGL 2. It runs the shareware Deus Ex demo

Report any issues on the repo: github.com/jarmitage99/OpenDE

Controls

WASD to move, LMB to fire, RMB to use. Tab toggles the inventory and ESC the main menu.

Scroll up to walk backward; scroll down to walk forward. Keep scrolling to keep moving.

Scroll left to turn right; scroll right to turn left. Turning is slower. Scroll diagonally to move and turn at the same time.

Hold C to crouch, X to toggle crouch, T to throw/drop, Y to equip/unequip your weapon, F/V for next/previous weapon/item, R to reload, Z to zoom.

On touchscreens, drag one finger right away to move or turn. Hold still to use, then drag to look around. With two fingers, drag the left finger to move or strafe and the right finger to look. Tap to fire or select, or double-tap to jump forward.

Settings and saved games are kept in browser storage.
